The recommendation

Take Signal House
forward.

It gives Greg a recognisable identity and makes the portfolio’s navigation part of the idea. Named channels give visitors an immediate overview; the expanded window brings the project, technology and Greg’s contribution together.

The risk is that the television becomes a gimmick. Keep the tuning brief, the work legible and the plain project index one click away. Open Studio is the strongest alternative if the priority is a more technical, evidence-led impression.

Research behind the direction

Production Portugal pairs a dominant media stage with a persistent project index. Awake commits to bold typography, colour and physical objects. Poolsuite shows how a coherent retro interface can create character.

Real work, clearly attributed

Project descriptions are grounded in Greg’s CV and verified GitHub contributions. Website images are October 2026 captures. The Greg Hannan Studio image is a labelled architecture illustration based on its source.
